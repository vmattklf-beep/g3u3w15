What is a form?

A form is a set of questions used to collect information. Everyone answers the same questions.

⚠️ Disadvantage: Someone may skip a question or enter a wrong answer.

💡 Task 4 · Learn · Databases

Paper or digital?

A database is an organized collection of information. It can be stored on paper or on a device.

📄

Paper database

Examples: a class attendance notebook or library borrowing cards.

✅ Works without a computer.

⚠️ Takes space and searching by hand may be slow.

💻

Digital database

Examples: an Excel book list or a school's library computer system.

✅ Easier to search and sort many records.

⚠️ Needs a working device, and some systems need internet.

Remember: Both store information. Not every digital database needs the internet.

Next, you'll sort examples into the correct database group.
